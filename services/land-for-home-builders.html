Land for Home Builders

Terra Nova Equity sits between two groups that need each other: landowners holding buildable lots they will never develop, and home builders who need finished lot inventory in the right submarkets. We buy the land for cash and place it with builders who can build on it.

Sell Us Your Lot I Am a Builder

For Landowners: Sell a Lot That Suits a Builder

If you own a residential lot in or near a growing submarket, you are holding something a builder wants. The problem is that builders do not browse listings the way homebuyers do. They work from their own pipeline, their own buy box, and their own entitlement schedule. A lot that would be perfect for them can sit on the MLS for two years because they never saw it.

We solve that by buying the lot outright. You get cash and a closing date. We take on the work of matching it to a builder, handling any cleanup, and holding it until the right project comes along.

What Makes a Lot Builder-Ready

  • Legal access. Frontage on a public road or a recorded, deeded easement. Not a handshake with the neighbor.
  • Utilities. Water and sewer at the street, or a viable well and septic plan. Electric nearby.
  • Zoning. Residential zoning that permits the product builders are actually selling in that submarket.
  • Topography and drainage. Buildable pad, no floodplain, no wetlands, and soils that will support a foundation.
  • Plat and survey. A recorded plat and a current survey remove weeks of uncertainty.
  • Price. A basis that leaves room for construction cost and builder margin.

If your lot is missing one of these, it is not automatically a no. It means the offer reflects the cost of solving it. We will tell you which items are the problem and what they do to the number.

Lots We Buy

  • Single infill lots in established subdivisions
  • Multi-lot parcels and small platted sections
  • Inherited lots held by out-of-state heirs
  • Lots with back taxes or open permits
  • Lots in growth corridors where new construction is active
  • Raw acreage in the path of residential development

For Builders: Lot Inventory Without the Land Hunt

Finding lots is one of the least scalable parts of a home building business. You either pay a land broker, chase expired listings, or spend your own time cold-calling owners who may not want to sell. Meanwhile your construction crew needs the next start.

We buy lots directly from owners, which means we can offer them to builders without a retail markup layered on top of a broker commission. Tell us your buy box and we will only send parcels that fit it.

What to Send Us

  • Target submarkets and counties
  • Lot size and frontage requirements
  • Price range per lot
  • Zoning and utility requirements
  • How many starts per year you can absorb
  • Whether you need platted, permitted, or raw land

How We Work With Builders

  • We buy the lot first, so there is no assignment risk
  • Clean title delivered through a title company
  • Survey and plat provided where available
  • Pricing based on your build cost, not a retail comp
  • Repeat supply in submarkets where we are active

Why We Focus on Buildable Land

Buying land is only useful if someone can build on it. That is why we are strict about access, utilities, and zoning. A parcel with no legal access is a liability, not an asset, no matter how cheap it is. A parcel with utilities at the street and residential zoning in an active submarket is a product.

That focus also makes us a better buyer for landowners. We are not guessing at value. We know what a builder will pay for a lot in a given subdivision, because we are talking to builders there every week. That lets us make a real offer quickly instead of tying your property up while we shop it around.
