What Makes Southern Indiana Different

Floyd and Clark counties have some of the oldest housing stock in the Louisville metro. New Albany and Jeffersonville both have large historic districts where the houses are beautiful, the maintenance is expensive, and the renovation rules are strict. A retail buyer sees charm and a project. A lender sees an appraisal risk.

At the same time, the riverfront and the I-265 corridor have attracted new investment, and land values in the right locations have moved. That means an older house on a good lot may be worth more as a redevelopment site than as a rental.

We underwrite both the structure and the lot, and we tell you which one is driving the offer. We also know the difference between a Floyd County closing and a Clark County one, and we use Indiana title companies rather than trying to force a Kentucky process across the river.

Who Calls Us in Southern Indiana

  • Owners of older historic-district homes. Properties with deferred maintenance and renovation rules that make a retail sale complicated.
  • Out-of-state heirs. Inherited homes in New Albany or Jeffersonville that nobody local wants to manage.
  • Tired landlords. Small rental portfolios in Clarksville and Sellersburg that have become more work than income.
  • Estate and probate sales. Properties that need to be liquidated on a court timeline.
  • Landowners. Families holding buildable lots or small acreage in Harrison County.

Areas We Cover

In Floyd County we cover New Albany, Floyds Knobs, Georgetown, and Greenville. In Clark County we cover Jeffersonville, Clarksville, Sellersburg, Charlestown, Henryville, and Borden. In Harrison County we cover Corydon, Corydon Junction, and the surrounding rural areas.

How the Offer Works

Send the address and a few details. We pull the county property record, look at recent sales nearby, estimate the repair scope, and check title. Then we send a written offer with the price, the closing costs we cover, and a timeline.

No obligation, no fee to see the number, and no pressure. If listing with an agent is the better path, we will say so.

Cash Sale vs. Listing in Southern Indiana

Selling to Terra Nova

  • Offer in about 24 hours
  • Close in 7 to 21 days, or later if you choose
  • No repairs, cleaning, or showings
  • No commissions or closing costs on your side
  • No financing or appraisal contingency

Listing With an Agent

  • Weeks to prepare, list, and find a buyer
  • 30 to 60 days to close after going under contract
  • Repairs and staging often required
  • 5 to 6 percent commission plus closing costs
  • Deals can fail on appraisal or financing

A move-in-ready house in a desirable Southern Indiana neighborhood will usually net more through an agent. A cash offer wins when the house needs work, the timeline is short, or the seller is not local. We will tell you which one fits your situation.
