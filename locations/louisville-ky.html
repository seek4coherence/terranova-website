Why Louisville Sellers Call Us

Louisville's housing stock is old. A large share of the metro was built before 1970, which means knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing, clay sewer laterals, and foundations that have had sixty years to move. Those are not cosmetic problems, and they are exactly the items that turn a retail sale into a renegotiation.

We also see a lot of inherited property. Families hold onto a house in Portland or Shawnee or Newburg for years after the last occupant leaves, paying taxes and insurance while the roof gets worse. By the time they decide to sell, the repair list is longer than the value of the house.

We buy those properties as they sit. No repair list, no inspection negotiation, no cleanout. You get a written offer and a closing date, and we take on the work.

Louisville-Specific Issues We Handle

  • Open code enforcement cases. Metro Louisville citations for overgrowth, structural deficiency, and vacant property registration.
  • Condemned structures. Properties posted as unsafe, including ones already scheduled for demolition.
  • Clay sewer laterals. Failed laterals that show up in a retail buyer's inspection and kill the deal.
  • Flood-prone areas. Properties near the Ohio River and in low-lying areas with repeated water intrusion.
  • Inherited and probate property. Estates in Jefferson County probate court where the heirs live out of state.
  • Vacant infill lots. Empty parcels in established neighborhoods that are ready for new construction.

How a Louisville Sale Works

  1. Submit the address. We pull the Jefferson County property record and look at recent sales nearby.
  2. We walk the property. Because we are local, we can usually see it within a few days rather than relying on photos.
  3. You get a written offer. Usually within 24 hours of the walkthrough.
  4. Close with a local title company. We use Kentucky title companies that know Jefferson County recording and lien practice.

You do not need to clean, repair, or move anything before closing. Take what you want and leave the rest.
