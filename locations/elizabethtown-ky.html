What Makes Hardin County Different

Elizabethtown sits at the intersection of I-65 and the Western Kentucky Parkway, and the corridor has absorbed a wave of industrial investment over the last several years. That growth pulls in workers, and workers need housing. At the same time, a large share of the existing stock in Radcliff and Vine Grove was built decades ago for military families stationed at Fort Knox.

The result is a market with two speeds. New construction on the edges sells quickly. Older homes with deferred maintenance sit, because a retail buyer sees a repair list and a lender sees an appraisal problem. That is exactly the gap a cash buyer fills.

It also means the land underneath those older homes is often worth more than the structure. We underwrite both, and we will tell you which one is driving the number.

Who Calls Us in Hardin County

  • Military families with a PCS order. A report date does not wait for a listing, a showing schedule, or a buyer's loan approval.
  • Out-of-state heirs. Inherited homes in Radcliff or Vine Grove that nobody local wants to manage or repair.
  • Tired landlords. Small rental portfolios near Fort Knox that have become more work than income.
  • Older homes with structural problems. Settled foundations, failing roofs, and systems that have outlived their service life.
  • Estate and probate sales. Properties that need to be liquidated on a court timeline.

Cash Sale vs. Listing in Elizabethtown

Selling to Terra Nova

  • Offer in about 24 hours
  • Close in 7 to 21 days, or later if you choose
  • No repairs, cleaning, or showings
  • No commissions or closing costs on your side
  • No financing or appraisal contingency

Listing With an Agent

  • Weeks to prepare, list, and find a buyer
  • 30 to 60 days to close after going under contract
  • Repairs and staging often required
  • 5 to 6 percent commission plus closing costs
  • Deals can fail on appraisal or financing

A move-in-ready house in a desirable Elizabethtown subdivision will usually net more through an agent. A cash offer wins when the house needs work, the timeline is short, or the seller is not local. We will tell you which one fits your situation.
