Local knowledge is the difference between an offer that closes and an offer that falls apart in week three.

What We Buy in Every Market

The property types do not change much from city to city. What changes is the local reason a seller needs out.

  • Distressed houses. Fire and water damage, failed roofs, foundation problems, hoarder conditions, and long vacancies.
  • Inherited and probate property. Out-of-state heirs, unsettled estates, and houses full of belongings nobody wants to move.
  • Pre-foreclosure and tax-delinquent property. Auction dates and tax sales that a retail listing cannot beat.
  • Tired rentals. Non-paying tenants, deferred maintenance, and landlords who are done with the unit.
  • Vacant land. Infill lots, buildable parcels, landlocked acreage, and land with back taxes or open violations.

In every case the offer is based on the property as it sits today. No repair list, no cleanout, no showings, and no financing contingency.
