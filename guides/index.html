Start With the Situation, Not the Sale

A house is the largest asset most families own, and the decisions around it are usually made under time pressure and emotional strain. The worst outcomes happen when a seller signs the first contract put in front of them because nobody explained the alternatives.

Each guide below covers the same three things: what the situation actually is, what the realistic options are, and what a cash sale does and does not solve. Where the answer depends on a lawyer or a tax professional, we say so instead of guessing.
