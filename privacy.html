Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Terra Nova Equity LLC ("Terra Nova," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.terranovaequityllc.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit a property or land inquiry, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The property or lot address, including parcel identifiers where provided
  • Property details such as type, condition, size, zoning, and asking price
  • Any additional details you choose to share in the message field

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as pages visited, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser type, and device type, through Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To evaluate your property and prepare a cash offer
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry
  • To perform due diligence, including property records and title research
  • To improve our website, services, and marketing
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate our business, including form processing, analytics, title companies, closing attorneys, and contractors involved in evaluating or purchasing a property. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect aggregated usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your browser settings to block cookies.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to evaluate your property, complete a transaction, and meet our legal and accounting requirements.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you submitted by emailing us at jimmy@terranovaequityllc.com. You may also ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you submit. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
